Chat you actually control.

A small, self-hosted chat service. Channels, threads and direct messages — with end-to-end encryption where it counts, and a plain account of what it does and doesn't protect.

What it is

Someone you know runs this server. That's the whole model.

There are no accounts to buy, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers anywhere in the app. Nothing you write is mined, profiled or sold, because there's no business here to sell it to. Open Relay is free software under the GNU GPL-3.0 — anyone can read exactly what it does.

Features

Everything you'd expect from a chat app.

# Channels & DMs

Public and private channels, plus one-to-one direct messages. Close a DM to tidy your sidebar without deleting anything.

🧵 Threads & replies

Branch a side conversation into a thread, or reply inline with the original quoted above your message.

🔴 Unread & mentions

Unread counts on every channel, with a highlighted badge when someone actually mentions you.

📎 Files & images

Drag a file anywhere to send it. Images preview inline; documents arrive as a download card.

😀 Reactions & GIFs

React with emoji, and search GIFs without your address ever being handed to the GIF provider.

🔔 Notifications

Get notified about direct messages and mentions, even with the app closed. Notifications say who and where, never what.

✍️ Formatting

Bold, italics, strikethrough, inline code and fenced code blocks — so pasted code stays readable.

🔍 Search

Search back through everything you can see. Encrypted DMs are excluded — the server can't read them to index them.

🟢 Presence

See who's around, who's typing and who's away — or switch all of that off and use Open Relay invisibly.

🛠️ Moderation

Channel owners and operators can invite, kick, ban and promote, with every privileged action written to an audit log.

📱 Works on your phone

A proper mobile layout with swipe navigation — swipe back to your channels, swipe across for the member list.

📲 Install it

Add it to your home screen or desktop and it opens in its own window, like any other app.

🎯 Find your place

Search jumps straight to the message and highlights it, and half-written replies are kept when you switch channels.

🔑 Sign in your way

Use a username and password, or sign in with Discord. Either way you can set or change a password later.

Privacy

Protections that hold up, described precisely.

  • 🔒 End-to-end encrypted direct messages Turn it on and your browser generates a key that never leaves your device. DMs are encrypted before they're sent, so the server stores text it has no way to unlock — the person running it genuinely cannot read them.
  • 🗂️ Encrypted files, not just messages Files shared in an encrypted DM are encrypted too. The server doesn't store the contents, the filename, or even the file type — only an anonymous blob.
  • 📍 Photos don't carry your location Every image is re-encoded in your browser before upload, which strips EXIF data including GPS coordinates. If that processing fails, the upload is refused rather than sending the original.
  • 🎛️ Controls the server actually honours Hide your typing, appear offline, block new DMs, or stay out of user search. Each is enforced server-side, so switching one off stops the signal being produced — not just hidden on your screen.
  • 🔢 You can verify the encryption Every encrypted conversation shows a safety number. Read it aloud to the other person — if it matches, nobody is intercepting, not even whoever runs the server.
  • 📦 Take your data, or delete it Download everything the server holds about you as a file, or delete your account outright, from your own profile. No asking permission.
  • 🚫 No tracking of any kind No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts, no tracking cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in and nothing else.
  • 🧹 Deleting really deletes Deleted messages disappear immediately and are erased from the database after 30 days, along with any files left behind.
  • 🔑 Sensible account security Passwords are hashed with Argon2 and never stored in the clear. Changing yours signs out every other device immediately.

And what it doesn't protect

  • Channel messages are readable by whoever runs the server. Only direct messages can be end-to-end encrypted.
  • Encryption hides what you said, not that you said it — who you message and when is still recorded.
  • Files shared in ordinary channels sit behind an unguessable but public link. Anyone given that link can open it.
  • Forget your encryption passphrase and those messages are gone for everyone, permanently. There is no reset, and no point asking whoever runs the server — the key they hold is locked with your passphrase, so they have nothing that can open it either.
