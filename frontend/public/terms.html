Last updated: 24 July 2026

Open Relay is a small chat service run by one person on their own server. It is free, non-commercial, and provided as-is. These terms are written in plain English rather than legalese — they describe what is expected of you, and what you can and can't expect in return.

Who provides this service

This instance is operated privately by an individual, not a company. There is no support desk, no service agreement and no organisation standing behind it. Questions, complaints and account requests go to the person who runs the server — the site administrator.

Using an account

  • You need an account to use the service. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Don't impersonate other people, and don't create accounts to evade a ban.
  • If you believe someone else has access to your account, change your password — that signs out every other device.
  • You must be old enough to agree to these terms where you live.

What you may not do

Don't use Open Relay to:

  • break the law, or share content that is illegal where you or the server are located;
  • harass, threaten, bully or target other people;
  • share sexual content involving minors, or content promoting violence against others — this results in an immediate and permanent ban;
  • distribute malware, phishing links, or spam;
  • upload material you have no right to share;
  • attack the service itself — probing for holes, overwhelming it, or trying to reach other people's data.

Finding a security flaw is welcome; exploiting it is not. Report it to the administrator rather than using it.

Your content

What you write stays yours. You keep ownership of your messages and files. By posting, you give the operator permission to store and transmit that content for the sole purpose of running the service — so it can be delivered to the people you're talking to and shown back to you later. Nothing is sold, published, or used to train anything.

You are responsible for what you post. Note that a message you delete disappears for everyone straight away but is fully erased on a delay, and that content in ordinary channels is readable by the operator. The privacy policy covers exactly what is stored and who can see it.

Moderation

Administrators and channel operators can remove messages, kick, and ban people. There is no formal appeals process — this is a small service run by a person, not a platform with a moderation department. Decisions are made at their discretion, and accounts can be suspended or removed if these terms are broken.

This service comes with no warranty of any kind. It may be slow, may break, may lose messages, and may be shut down or wiped at any time without notice. Do not rely on it as the only place any important information exists, and keep your own copies of anything that matters. To the fullest extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for any loss or damage arising from your use of it.

Encrypted messages carry a specific risk worth repeating: if you forget your encryption passphrase, those messages cannot be recovered by anyone, including the administrator.

Ending things

You can stop using Open Relay whenever you like. Your profile has a button to delete your account outright, and another to download a copy of your data first. The operator may likewise suspend or remove an account, or discontinue the service entirely.
